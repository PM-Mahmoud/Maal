# HECS-HELP Student Debt

HECS-HELP (Higher Education Contribution Scheme — Higher Education Loan Program) is the Australian government loan that covers the cost of Commonwealth-supported university places. You defer payment and repay it through the tax system once your income exceeds the minimum repayment threshold.

## How HECS-HELP Works

When you enrol in a Commonwealth-supported place, you can choose to pay your student contribution upfront or defer it via HECS-HELP. If you defer, the government pays your contribution directly to the university, and you incur a HECS-HELP debt.

**There is no interest on HECS-HELP debt in the traditional sense.** Instead, the debt is indexed annually on 1 June using the Consumer Price Index (CPI), which means it grows with inflation. If inflation is high, your debt grows faster.

## Repayment Thresholds (2025-26)

Repayments are made automatically through the tax system once your income reaches the minimum threshold. Repayment rates increase with income.

- Below $54,435: 0% (no repayment)
- $54,435 – $62,850: 1.0%
- $62,851 – $66,620: 2.0%
- $66,621 – $70,618: 2.5%
- $70,619 – $74,855: 3.0%
- $74,856 – $79,346: 3.5%
- $79,347 – $84,107: 4.0%
- $84,108 – $89,154: 4.5%
- $89,155 – $94,504: 5.0%
- $94,505 – $100,174: 5.5%
- $100,175 – $106,185: 6.0%
- $106,186 – $112,556: 6.5%
- $112,557 – $119,309: 7.0%
- $119,310 – $126,467: 7.5%
- Above $126,467: 8.0% or higher

## Key Considerations

**Voluntary repayments:** You can make voluntary repayments at any time via myGov or the ATO. There is no discount for doing so (the 5% voluntary repayment bonus was abolished in 2017). However, voluntary repayments can be worthwhile when CPI indexation is high, as reducing the principal early limits future indexation.

**Overseas repayment:** If you move overseas, you must still repay your HECS-HELP debt if your worldwide income exceeds the repayment threshold. You self-assess and pay via the ATO.

**Impact on borrowing:** HECS-HELP repayments are included in lenders' serviceability assessments for home loans. A large HECS debt can reduce your borrowing capacity.

**Medical and law students:** These degrees typically generate the largest HECS-HELP debts — often $80,000–$120,000 for a full medical degree. Understanding how indexation affects the total cost is important for financial planning.

## HECS vs Private Loans

HECS-HELP is generally superior to commercial student loans: no real interest, income-contingent repayments, no collection action if income falls below threshold. There is rarely a reason to take a commercial loan to pay off HECS early unless you expect very high inflation for an extended period.
