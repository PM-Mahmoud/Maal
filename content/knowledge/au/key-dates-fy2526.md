## Financial Year

Australia's financial year runs **1 July to 30 June**. FY2025-26 = 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

## Annual Key Dates

### January
- **1 Jan**: HECS-HELP debt accrues interest from 1 July — indexation calculation reference period begins
- **28 Jan**: Q2 PAYG instalment due (businesses, some individuals)
- **31 Jan**: Lodge tax return if you have a tax agent (some clients — check with agent)

### February
- **28 Feb**: Late lodgement deadline for some tax returns under agent arrangements

### March
- **31 Mar**: Due date for most tax agents to lodge prior-year returns (extended from Oct under agent)

### April
- **28 Apr**: Q3 PAYG instalment due
- **30 Apr**: Deadline to lodge FBT return (Fringe Benefits Tax, 1 Apr–31 Mar FBT year)

### May
- **15 May**: Tax agents' extended lodgement deadline for most prior-year individual returns
- **31 May**: Super Guarantee charge statement due if Q3 SG not paid on time

### June
- **1 Jun**: **HECS-HELP annual indexation** — outstanding HECS debt indexed by CPI (June 2025 CPI used for 1 June 2025 indexation). Indexation rate for 1 June 2025: **3.2%** (down from 4.7% in 2024)
- **15 Jun**: Q4 PAYG instalment due (some entities)
- **25 Jun**: Last date to make a **concessional super contribution** and have it processed by 30 June for current-year tax deduction (allow 3–5 business days processing)
- **30 Jun**: **End of Financial Year (EOFY)**
  - Last day to make concessional or non-concessional super contributions counting to this FY
  - Last day to realise capital gains/losses for this FY
  - Last day to make charitable donations deductible this FY
  - Super co-contribution eligibility cutoff (income threshold assessed this FY)
  - Spouse contribution offset assessed this FY
  - Last day to prepay investment loan interest (up to 12 months ahead) for an immediate deduction

### July
- **1 Jul**: New financial year begins
- **1 Jul**: **Super Guarantee rate increases** (see below for current rate)
- **1 Jul**: New income tax thresholds apply
- **1 Jul**: New super contribution caps apply
- **1 Jul**: HECS repayment thresholds reset (based on prior-year tax return)
- **28 Jul**: Q4 SG (April–June) must be paid to super fund by this date
- **31 Jul**: Lodge quarterly BAS for Q4 (large/medium businesses)

### August
- **14 Aug**: Q4 BAS due (medium businesses with tax agent)
- **28 Aug**: Lodge and pay Q4 BAS for smaller businesses (ATO online)

### October
- **28 Oct**: Q1 PAYG instalment due (July–September)
- **31 Oct**: **Individual tax return lodgement deadline** (if no tax agent) for prior financial year

### November
- **28 Nov**: Q1 BAS due (if using quarterly reporting, July–September)

### December
- **1 Dec**: Last day to make contributions to preserve prior-year carry-forward concessional cap entitlements (some conditions)

## Super Guarantee Rates (Superannuation Guarantee)

| Financial Year | SG Rate |
|---|---|
| FY2021-22 | 10.0% |
| FY2022-23 | 10.5% |
| FY2023-24 | 11.0% |
| FY2024-25 | 11.5% |
| FY2025-26 | **12.0%** (final scheduled rate) |

From 1 July 2025, the SG rate is permanently 12%. No further scheduled increases.

## HECS-HELP Indexation History

| Year | Indexation Rate |
|---|---|
| 1 June 2021 | 0.6% |
| 1 June 2022 | 3.9% |
| 1 June 2023 | 7.1% |
| 1 June 2024 | 4.7% |
| 1 June 2025 | 3.2% |

Indexation is based on the lower of the CPI or Wage Price Index (WPI). The 2025 rate was 3.2%. For FY2026 (1 June 2026), the rate will be announced mid-2026 based on March 2026 CPI/WPI figures.

## Quarterly BAS / PAYG Dates (Standard)

| Quarter | Period | Due Date |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | July–September | 28 October |
| Q2 | October–December | 28 February |
| Q3 | January–March | 28 April |
| Q4 | April–June | 28 July |

## Key ATO Lodgement Dates Summary

- **31 October**: Individual tax return (self-lodging, no tax agent)
- **15 May**: Most returns via registered tax agent
- **1 June**: HECS indexation
- **30 June**: EOFY — all year-end transactions
- **28 July**: SG contributions for April–June quarter

## Medicare Levy Surcharge — Date Impact

The Medicare Levy Surcharge (MLS) is assessed annually based on your taxable income + reportable fringe benefits + total net investment losses. It applies if you don't have adequate private hospital cover for the full year. Getting hospital cover before 1 July avoids MLS for the entire following financial year.
