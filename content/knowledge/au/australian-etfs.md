# Australian ETFs

Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs) listed on the ASX have transformed how Australians invest. They offer low-cost diversification across Australian and global markets, accessible from any brokerage account.

## What is an ETF?

An ETF is a fund that trades on a stock exchange, typically tracking an index. Unlike managed funds, ETFs can be bought and sold throughout the trading day at market prices. Most ETFs are passive — they aim to replicate the performance of an index, not beat it.

## Major Australian ETF Providers

- **Vanguard**: One of the world's largest asset managers, known for ultra-low cost index funds
- **Betashares**: Australia-based ETF provider with a wide range of products
- **iShares (BlackRock)**: Global ETF giant with strong ASX presence
- **VanEck**: Specialises in smart-beta and thematic ETFs
- **SPDR (State Street)**: One of the original ETF managers globally

## Management Expense Ratio (MER)

The MER is the annual fee expressed as a percentage of your investment. A $100,000 investment in an ETF with 0.07% MER costs $70 per year in fees — far less than most managed funds (which typically charge 0.5%–2%). Over decades, lower fees compound into significantly better returns.

## Currency Hedging

Some international ETFs hedge currency exposure (e.g., VGAD — Vanguard hedged global shares). Hedged ETFs reduce the impact of AUD/USD fluctuations but have slightly higher costs. Unhedged ETFs (VGS) benefit from AUD weakness and suffer from AUD strength.

## Tax Considerations

ETFs are tax-efficient structures. They pass through dividends and franking credits from underlying shares. Capital gains are only crystallised when you sell your ETF units — not when the underlying index rebalances.
